This isn't a black box. Here's exactly what happens to your voice.

Most "AI voice analysis" products won't tell you how the score got made. We will — because we built it ourselves, and it's simpler, and more honest, than it sounds.

01

Pitch extraction

Your recording runs through the YIN algorithm — a published pitch-detection method (de Cheveigné & Kawahara, 2002) — implemented from scratch in Python and numpy, frame by frame, to trace your actual pitch in Hz. No AI model touches this step. It's pure signal processing.

02

Feature extraction

From that pitch trace and the raw waveform: pitch error in cents, pitch jitter, onset-to-onset rhythm spacing, RMS loudness and dynamic range, the H1–H2 harmonic ratio, and singer's-formant energy (the 2.5–3.5kHz band that determines how forward or covered a voice sounds).

03

Six-axis scoring

Those numbers map onto your radar chart against thresholds set from real coaching experience — not a machine-learning model. Only then does AI enter: Claude writes the plain-language explanation of what your numbers mean.

BREATH

Measured directly from your long-tone task (duration + stability) when you record one — otherwise approximated from your dynamics score.

RESONANCE

Singer's-formant energy ratio — how much of your sound concentrates in the band that reads as "forward" versus "buried."

PITCH LINK

Pitch stability, plus — when available — how smoothly you connect notes on the low-to-high siren task.

DYNAMICS

RMS loudness variation and dynamic range, measured directly across your recording.

ARTICULATION

Approximated from rhythm and dynamics data — there's no dedicated diction task yet, so this one partly leans on your own self-rating when you give one.

DELIVERY

Same approach as Articulation — approximated, and blended with your self-reported answer when you provide it.

This is a rule-based signal-processing engine we designed ourselves, not a deep-learning model. The scoring thresholds come from real vocal-coaching experience, and calibrating them against real judge data is on our roadmap. The one place generative AI actually runs is turning your numbers into a report that reads like a person wrote it.

Four axes. Your combination is your Voice Type.

Think MBTI, but for how you actually sound. Each axis is a spectrum between two poles — where you land on all four shapes which genres, techniques and repertoire actually fit your voice.

Breath–Voice ContactBalanced ↔ Pressed
Resonance FocusForward ↔ Rear
Pitch ConnectionConnected ↔ Disconnected
Expression DynamicsDynamic ↔ Static
PRESSED · FORWARD · CONNECTED · DYNAMIC

The Belter

Anthemic pop and dance-pop choruses built for big, forward-placed power notes — the sound of a stadium K-pop title track.

BALANCED · FORWARD · CONNECTED · DYNAMIC

The Runner

Contemporary R&B and soul, where smooth note-to-note connection carries runs and ad-libs without strain.

BALANCED · REAR · CONNECTED · STATIC

The Storyteller

Ballads and acoustic singer-songwriter material, where a warm, held-back tone lets the lyric carry the song.

PRESSED · REAR · DISCONNECTED · DYNAMIC

The Edge

Rock and rap-rock delivery, where deliberate staccato attack and grit read as energy, not a flaw.

These four are examples, not the full set — four axes with two poles each makes sixteen real combinations, and most voices sit somewhere between the poles rather than at one extreme. Your actual VBTI report shows exactly where you land on each spectrum, in percentages.
